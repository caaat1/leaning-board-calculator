Board · support · span

Leaning Board Calculator

A board of length L and thickness T leans at angle ∠BAF across span S between two verticals, resting on a support of thickness t. Enter any two of L, S and ∠BAF, and the page works out the third along with every segment of the layout. Use any length unit, as long as it's the same for all lengths. A decimal point or a decimal comma both work.
