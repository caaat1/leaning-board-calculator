Board · support · span

Leaning Board Calculator

A board of length L and thickness T leans at angle ∠BAF across span S between two verticals, resting on a support of thickness t. Enter any two of L, S, H and ∠BAF, and the page works out the other two along with every segment of the layout. Use any length unit, as long as it's the same for all lengths. A decimal point or a decimal comma both work.

Formulas

Calculated values

θ = atan2(TS + Lr, LS − Tr) θ = atan2(LH − Tr, TH + Lr) θ: H cos θ − S sin θ = T cos 2θ
L = (S − T sin θ) / cos θ L = (H − T cos θ) / sin θ S = L cos θ + T sin θ H = L sin θ + T cos θ

where r = √(L² + T² − S²) with S entered, or √(L² + T² − H²) with H entered. With S and H entered, θ is solved numerically.

Segments

AF = L cos θ = S − AA′ BF = L sin θ = H − BC′ AA′ = CC′ = T sin θ DA′ = BC′ = T cos θ FG = BF(AF − t) / AF BG = BF − FG = t tan θ
