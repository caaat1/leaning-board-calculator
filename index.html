Board · support · span

Leaning Board Calculator

A board of length L and thickness T leans at angle θ across span S between two verticals, resting on a support of thickness t.
Enter any two of L, S, H, h and θ, and the page works out the rest along with every segment of the layout.
Use any length unit, as long as it's the same for all lengths.
A decimal point or a decimal comma both work.

Formulas

Calculated values

θ = atan2(TS + Lr, LS − Tr) θ = atan2(LH − Tr, TH + Lr) θ: solved numerically from the two values
L = (S − T sin θ) / cos θ L = (H − T cos θ) / sin θ L = (h + t tan θ) / sin θ S = L cos θ + T sin θ H = L sin θ + T cos θ h = L sin θ − t tan θ

where r = √(L² + T² − S²) with S entered, or √(L² + T² − H²) with H entered. Other pairs without θ are solved numerically.

Segments

AF = L cos θ = S − AA′ BF = L sin θ = H − BC′ AA′ = CC′ = T sin θ DA′ = BC′ = T cos θ BG = BF − h = t tan θ
